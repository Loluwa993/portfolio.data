Cafe Harmony Sales & Business Insight Dashboard

Overview 📈🔬:

Developed an interactive sales analysis dashboard in Excel for Cafe Harmony, a multi-location café chain, aimed at uncovering key performance drivers, optimizing operations, and enhancing customer experience.

  • File: Cafe Harmony Analysis Report

Key Features

  • Sales Analysis: Year-over-year and monthly trends, with breakdowns by product and location
  • Customer Insights: Age and gender demographics driving sales, with product preference rankings
  • Employee Performance: Target vs. actual sales by job role and location
  • Stock & Inventory Analysis: Identification of top-moving items and understock issues
  • Customer Feedback Dashboard: Analysis of satisfaction ratings and sentiment breakdown using visual metrics

Tools Used

  • Microsoft Excel (Pivot Tables, Charts, Conditional Formatting, Form Controls)
  • Data Visualization Best Practices
  • Basic Analytics Techniques (Trend analysis, KPI benchmarking)

Impact

  • Revealed that Uptown drives highest sales
  • Identified Muffins & Cappuccinos as top-selling products
  • Uncovered service quality gaps through 69.7% neutral/negative feedback
  • Informed strategy for A/B testing, training improvements, and inventory control

Conclusion 📌:

This analysis is strategized to enable stakeholders to make data-informed decisions on promotions, staffing, and product focus, ultimately driving a more customer-centric business approach.
